HOW TO PLAY

RULES & STRATEGY

🎯 THE OBJECTIVE

Guess the hidden 5-letter word before you run out of attempts.

You have a maximum of 6 guesses to solve the puzzle.

🧩 HOW TO PLAY

Enter a valid 5-letter word and submit your guess.

After every guess, the tiles change colour. Use the colours as clues to find the hidden word.

🎨 UNDERSTAND THE COLOURS

🟩 GREEN — The letter is in the word and is in the correct position.

🟨 YELLOW — The letter is in the word but is in the wrong position.

⬛ GREY — The letter is not in the word.

🔁 DUPLICATE LETTERS

A hidden word can contain the same letter more than once.

Pay attention to each individual tile when guessing words with repeated letters.

🧠 STRATEGY

Try to make your first guesses informative. Words containing several different common letters can help reveal useful clues.

Including common vowels and consonants can help you identify which letters are present in the word.

Avoid repeatedly guessing the same letters unless the clues suggest that doing so is useful.

🔍 USE YOUR CLUES

Keep green letters in their confirmed positions.

Move yellow letters into positions that you have not tested yet.

Avoid letters that have been confirmed as absent.

Every guess should give you more information about the answer.

⚡ DON'T WASTE GUESSES

If you have several possible answers, consider making a guess that tests several unknown letters.

Sometimes a guess is useful even when you know it is not the answer because it can eliminate multiple possibilities.

📅 DAILY PUZZLE

The Daily Puzzle gives you the WordRush challenge for the day.

If you leave the Daily Puzzle before finishing, your progress can be resumed when you return.

Complete Daily Puzzles to build your Daily Streak 🔥.

💡 HINT

If you get stuck, you can use the Hint feature to receive additional information.

Use your hints strategically and try to solve the word using the clues you have already discovered.

🧠 THINK SMART.

🔍 USE EVERY CLUE.

🔥 KEEP YOUR STREAK ALIVE.
