PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Welcome to WordRush. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information WordRush collects, how that information is used, and the choices available to you when you use the WordRush game.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you create and use a WordRush account, we may collect the following information:

  • Email address — used for account registration and authentication.
  • Username — used to identify you within WordRush and on the leaderboard.
  • Game statistics — including games played, wins, losses, current streak, best streak, and total score.
  • Theme preference — your selected WordRush theme may be stored locally in your browser.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the WordRush game.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Information collected by WordRush is used to:

  • Create and manage your WordRush account.
  • Authenticate you when you sign in.
  • Save and display your game statistics.
  • Display your username and score on the WordRush leaderboard.
  • Maintain your gameplay experience and preferences.
  • Protect the service against misuse or unauthorized activity where reasonably necessary.

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to providing and operating WordRush unless we obtain an appropriate legal basis or otherwise notify you.

3. LEADERBOARD

WordRush includes a public leaderboard. If you create an account and participate in the leaderboard, your WordRush username and game-related statistics may be displayed to other WordRush users.

Your email address is not displayed publicly on the WordRush leaderboard.

4. HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS STORED

WordRush uses Supabase for user authentication and database services. Information associated with your WordRush account may therefore be stored using Supabase's infrastructure.

Access to account information is protected using authentication and database security controls. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

5. LOCAL STORAGE

WordRush may use your browser's local storage to remember certain preferences, such as your selected visual theme.

This information is stored on your device and is separate from your WordRush account data.

6. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

WordRush does not currently use advertising cookies or tracking cookies as part of the game's core functionality.

Third-party services used by WordRush may use their own technologies as described by their respective privacy policies.

7. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

WordRush currently uses third-party services to help operate the application, including Supabase for authentication and database functionality.

These third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. DATA RETENTION

We retain account and game information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide WordRush and maintain your account, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law.

9. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information. These may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used.

If you would like to make a privacy or data request, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

10. SOUTH AFRICAN PRIVACY LAW

If you are located in South Africa, the processing of your personal information may be subject to the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), where applicable.

WordRush aims to process personal information responsibly and only for legitimate purposes connected with operating and improving the service.

11. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

WordRush is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to WordRush without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

12. SECURITY

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with WordRush accounts. However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically is completely secure.

13. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as WordRush develops or as legal requirements change.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.

14. CONTACT US

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, your personal information, or your privacy rights, please contact the WordRush team.
